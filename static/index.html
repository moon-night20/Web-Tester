ADD TO YOUR WORKSPACE

Register a website

Enter a public hostname you control, without https:// or a page path. Localhost and IP addresses cannot be verified. You must be able to edit its DNS records.
STEP 1 OF 2 · WEBSITE OWNERSHIP

Add this DNS record

Open the DNS settings at the company where this domain's nameservers are managed (often your domain registrar or DNS provider). Create a TXT record with these values:

TYPETXT
HOST / NAME
VALUE / CONTENT
TTLDefault / Auto

If your DNS provider automatically adds the domain name, enter only _securelaunch-challenge as the host. Do not put this record in your website files or hosting settings. After saving, wait a few minutes for DNS to update.
